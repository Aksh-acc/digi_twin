INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------
Higher entropy_norm / lower hhi means a group's choices are less
predictable (closer to uniform over A/B/C/D). Fleiss' kappa quantifies
how much the 6 LLM agents agree with each other on identical tasks,
beyond chance. D_HA (mean JSD) is the flagship number: how much the
agent-trained twin's implied choice distribution diverges from the
human-trained twin's, on the same held-out decision tasks -- a value
of 0 would mean the twins are indistinguishable; this study's premise
is that it is meaningfully greater than 0.
