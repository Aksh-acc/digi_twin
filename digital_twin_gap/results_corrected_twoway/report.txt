INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------
A large positive gap means a twin built from one population is a poor
stand-in for the other. If agent-matched accuracy is much higher than
human-matched accuracy, it means LLM agents make far more predictable
(consistent, rule-like) choices than real people do -- so an 'agentic
digital twin' overstates how well it would model human consumers.
